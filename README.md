## Featured project: What Infection Rates Can Miss

Why might infection prevalence be lower in hospitals, while the population-level burden is higher? This project explores that question through estimates of five healthcare-associated infections in Germany and the EU/EEA, showing why one hospital rate can’t tell the whole story.

## Who this story is for

I wrote this with **public-hospital quality and safety managers** in mind. I look at why hospital prevalence and population burden can tell different parts of the story, and what that might mean when teams review infection-prevention priorities. The estimates cover five selected infections in Germany, so they are not a ready-made answer for every hospital. My hope is that the post encourages readers to look beyond one rate and ask what other evidence could help them see the fuller picture.
