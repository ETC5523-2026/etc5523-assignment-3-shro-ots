## Featured project: Why Lower Hospital Prevalence Doesn’t Always Mean Lower Burden

Why might infection prevalence be lower in hospitals, while population-level burden is higher? This project explores that question through estimates of five healthcare-associated infections in Germany and the EU/EEA, showing why one hospital rate can’t tell the whole story.

## Who this story is for

This blog is written for hospital infection-prevention and quality-and-safety managers who need a concise, non-technical explanation of how HAI frequency can differ from population-level health burden and what that means for prevention priorities.
